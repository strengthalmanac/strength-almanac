About the tools

Built by a lifter, for lifters

RPE-Based Warmup Calculator

The warmup calculator uses your last session's performance — weight, reps, and RPE — to calculate your estimated 1-rep max and generate a target weight for today's top set based on your goal reps and RPE. Warmup sets are then built as percentages of that target, specific to each lift.

Squats, bench press, sumo deadlift, and conventional deadlift each have their own loading percentages and cues — pause reps, positional work, and set-by-set rep targets — built from real competitive programming protocols used in USAPL powerlifting.

The RPE (Rate of Perceived Exertion) scale rates effort from 5 to 10, where 10 is maximum effort and 9 leaves one rep in the tank. Using RPE instead of fixed percentages accounts for daily readiness, sleep, fatigue, and stress — giving you a smarter target every session.

Plate Loader

The plate loader calculates exactly which plates to load for any target weight, displayed using official IPF and IWF color coding: red 25kg, blue 20kg, yellow 15kg, green 10kg, white 5kg, black 2.5kg, chrome 1.25kg. Supports standard Olympic bars, women's bars, custom bar weights, and accounts for collar weight including competition collars.

Plates are rounded to the nearest loadable increment — no more wondering if 187.3lbs is actually achievable on your bar.

Nutrition Timing Calculator

Strength training performance is heavily influenced by when you eat, not just how much. The nutrition timing planner takes your total daily protein, carbohydrate, and fat targets and distributes them across your day based on your training window — early morning, late morning, afternoon, or evening sessions.

Pre- and intra-workout carbohydrate recommendations are based on bodyweight-scaled guidelines. Meal distribution follows evidence-based sport nutrition protocols, with protein spread evenly across 6–7 meals and carbohydrates front-loaded around training. The last snack of the day is automatically removed at lower calorie intakes where it isn't needed.

The non-negotiables list covers the daily dietary foundations that support long-term health and recovery — fermented foods, omega-3s, micronutrient variety, and strategic supplementation.
