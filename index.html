The Tools
Warmup Calculator
Plug in your last session or a target weight and get a full warmup ramp built from real loading percentages, with rest times that scale to the lift and your RPE. Full or fast options depending on your time.
Plate Loader
Enter any weight and see exactly what to load, plate by plate, in kg or lbs. Handles custom bars and collars.
Nutrition Timing
Takes your daily macros and spreads them across the day around your training window, so you're fueled when it matters.
Supplements
Bodyweight-scaled dosing for the supplements actually worth taking. No fairy dust.
